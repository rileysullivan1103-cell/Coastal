# check_geometry_strict.py only, for its PRIMARY registration route:
# ORB/SIFT features plus a RANSAC homography fit. RANSAC is what rejects the
# moving water instead of averaging it in, and a homography is what makes a
# rotation or a zoom visible at all -- phase correlation measures a shift and
# is structurally blind to both. The headless build is the right one here:
# nothing in this repo opens a window, and the GUI build drags in Qt.
# Without it that script can still run --inventory and its phase route, but
# the phase route alone has nothing to cross-check it against.
